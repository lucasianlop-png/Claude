Questions

Do I need to know Notion?

No. If your team lives in Google Sheets, we use Sheets. Either way it's set up and ready, and the handover shows everyone how to find a drink.

Who owns the system?

You do. It's built inside your own Google or Notion account, and the menu files are yours once paid.

Are my recipes safe?

They're confidential, used only to build your system, and my working copies are deleted 30 days after delivery. It's in the agreement.

What do you need from me?

Photos of your recipe pages, any chats with specs, your current menu and logo, and someone who can answer questions within a couple of days.

Do you create new cocktails?

Not in these packages. I organise and design what you already pour. New recipe development can be quoted separately.

Summer menu still in the binder?

Tell me about your bar and I'll reply within a day with a fixed quote. No calls needed unless you want one.
