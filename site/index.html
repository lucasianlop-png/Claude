Questions

Do I need to know Notion?

No. If you can fill in a form, you can use it. Rather not touch it at all? I'll build it for you →

Are my recipes private?

Yes. The system lives in your own Notion workspace. I never see your recipes.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Notion has apps for iPhone and Android, so your team can open any spec mid-shift.

What if the menu changes?

Edit the card once and everyone sees the new spec straight away. Old specs get archived, not lost.

Who is it for?

Bartenders, bar managers and owners, from a small cocktail bar to a busy venue.
