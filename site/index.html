Same question, every shift

Every shift without a system costs you.

  • The same drink, different depending on who's on.
  • Hours of training, repeated for every new starter.
  • Prep binned because nobody wrote the date.
  • No time to look anything up on a Saturday rush.

The fix

One system. Every spec.

The Off the Binder System puts every recipe in the same clean format, on every phone behind the bar. Write it once and the whole team pours it the same way.

  • Specs, preps and batches each in their own format.
  • Yield, shelf life and date on every prep.
  • Any drink in seconds on the phone, mid-shift.

Built in Notion

A binder that searches itself.

Notion is a popular app for notes, docs and databases. In the system, every recipe is a page, every page sits in one tidy list, and the whole lot syncs to every phone behind the bar.

  • Free to start. Notion has a free plan, so there's no new subscription to get going.
  • On every device. Apps for iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows, plus any web browser.
  • Search, filter, share. Type two letters and the spec is there. Tap it and the full recipe opens.

Questions

    

      
Do I need to know Notion?
No. I set it up and train your team, so nobody has to learn it from scratch.

      
Are my recipes private?
Yes. The system lives in your own Notion workspace, and your recipes are never shared or used anywhere else.

      
Does it work on a phone?
Yes. Notion has apps for iPhone and Android, so your team can open any spec mid-shift.

      
What if the menu changes?
Edit the card once and everyone sees the new spec straight away. Old specs get archived, not lost.

      
Who is it for?
Bar managers and owners, from a small cocktail bar to a busy venue.

      
What about refunds?
Covered by the 14-day money-back guarantee. After that, your rights under the Australian Consumer Law still apply if anything's wrong with the product.
