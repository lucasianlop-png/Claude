Where your recipes actually live

Most bars run on three filing systems at once. None of them talk to each other, and all of them break on a busy Friday.

01

The binder

Sticky pages, crossed-out specs and a syrup recipe nobody has updated since the last menu change.

02

The group chat

The new spec got sent at 1 am, between a roster swap and a photo of someone's dinner. Good luck scrolling back to it.

03

Someone's head

It works perfectly, until that someone is on leave, on a day off or gone for good.

What lands every Tuesday

One story. One idea. Five minutes.

  • A story from behind the bar. The real stuff: what went wrong on a shift and what fixed it.
  • One system idea you can use this week. How to write a spec, label a prep, split production from service.
  • Smart tools, used properly. Where AI and good design save you time, and where they don't.

Two jobs, two cards

Production vs service

Prep recipes tell you how to make a batch. Service specs tell you what goes in the glass. Keep them apart and nobody asks what's in the house sour at 11 pm.
